Tesseract not found

DocuCloak uses Tesseract to read the text in scanned PDFs and images. Regular PDFs are redacted without it, but scanned PDFs and images will be listed as errors until it's installed.

1
Install Tesseract

With Homebrew: brew install tesseract

Or see other ways to install it.

2
Already installed?

DocuCloak looks on your PATH and in /opt/homebrew/bin and /usr/local/bin. If Tesseract is somewhere else, add its folder to your PATH in ~/.zshrc and restart DocuCloak:

export PATH="/path/to/tesseract/bin:$PATH"

3
Check again

After installing, click Check again or reload this page. The badge in the top-right corner turns green when Tesseract is found.
